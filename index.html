From leaf to cup

The usual path black tea takes through a factory like ours.

  1. Plucking

    Fresh green leaf is hand-picked from the estates and brought to the factory.

  2. Withering

    Leaf is spread out to lose moisture and become soft enough to roll.

  3. Rolling

    The leaf is rolled to break the cells and release its natural juices.

  4. Fermentation

    The rolled leaf rests so the flavour and colour develop.

  5. Drying

    Hot air stops fermentation and dries the tea.

  6. Sorting and packing

    The tea is graded by leaf size, then packed for dispatch.
